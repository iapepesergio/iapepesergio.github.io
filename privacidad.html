Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This application is a personal tool created by Pildoras de la Historia to record the statistics of its own TikTok account. It is not a public service and it does not offer any features to other users.

1. Data we access

With the explicit authorization of the account owner, the application reads from TikTok: the profile name, the number of followers, the total number of likes, the number of videos, and the public statistics of the videos (views and likes).

2. How we use the data

The data is used only to store these figures, once a day, in a private Google Sheets spreadsheet. Access to that spreadsheet is limited to the account owner and to the people the owner chooses to share it with.

3. Sharing of data

We do not sell, rent, transfer or disclose this data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and retention

The data and the access tokens are stored in Google services (Google Sheets and Google Apps Script) and are kept for as long as the tool is in use.

5. Revoking access and deleting data

The account owner can revoke the application's access at any time from the TikTok settings (Settings and privacy > Security & permissions > Apps and services) and can delete the stored data from the spreadsheet at any time.

6. Contact

For any question about this policy, write to iapepesergio@gmail.com.
